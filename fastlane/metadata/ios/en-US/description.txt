Emberwit is an AI assistant that runs entirely on your phone. Download a language model once, and every conversation after that happens on your device, even in airplane mode.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your chats never leave your phone. They are stored encrypted (AES-256).
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
• The internet is used only to download the model list and the models you choose.
• Optional app lock with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• A quick speed test checks what your phone can run, then recommends models that fit its memory and speed.
• Downloads are checked against a signed catalog, so you always get the exact model file.
• The app slows down when your phone is hot or the battery is low, and frees memory when other apps need it.

ENGLISH AND BENGALI
• The whole app is available in English and Bengali.
• Ask questions in either language.

FEATURES
• Streaming replies you can stop at any time
• Several conversations, search, pin and rename
• Choose a model per chat, adjust creativity and reply length
• Encrypted export and import of your chats with a passphrase
• Import your own GGUF model files

Models come from their creators (Qwen, Gemma, Llama, Phi and others), each under its own license, listed in the app. Answers from small on-device models can be wrong: check important information.
