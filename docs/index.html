Download once. Then it’s yours, offline.

Most AI apps send every word you type to a server far away. Emberwit puts the whole AI model inside your phone. The internet is needed one time, to download it.

  1. It checks your phone

    Memory, processor and a quick speed test, so it only suggests models your phone can run well.

  2. You download one model

    From 0.5 to 2.5 GB, over Wi-Fi if you like. Every file is checked against a signed list before it is used.

  3. Chat with no internet

    Airplane mode, load-shedding, no data pack. It answers anyway, because nothing has to leave the phone.

Speak to it. It can do things, too.

Tap the mic and talk instead of typing; your phone turns speech into text on the device. Ask for an alarm or the flashlight and Emberwit just does it, in English or Bengali, with no internet.

Voice typing stays on the phone. It uses your phone’s own offline speech recognizer, so your voice is never uploaded. English works everywhere; Bengali works if your phone offers it offline.

What happens on your phone stays on your phone.

Not a promise in a policy. It is how the app is built: there is no server to send your chats to.

What leaves your phone
Your messages and the repliesNever
Your voiceNever
Usage statistics, crash reports, ads IDNone collected
Name, email, phone numberNo account exists
Internet useOnly to fetch the model list and download the models you pick

Chats are encrypted

History is stored in an encrypted database, with the key kept in your phone’s secure storage.

Optional app lock

Open with your phone’s PIN or fingerprint. The screen is hidden from the recent-apps view while locked.

Backups you control

Export your chats as an encrypted file with your own password, and import them on another phone.

Even this website

No cookies, no analytics and no third-party requests. Fonts and code are served from this site only.

Gentle on your phone, honest about limits.

Phone gets hot
Emberwit slows down or pauses instead of cooking it.
Battery runs low
It takes it easy, and tells you why.
Memory runs short
The model is put away, and loaded again when you need it.

What it can’t do

  • It can’t search the web or know today’s news. It knows what the model learned.
  • Small models make mistakes. Check anything important.
  • The biggest models need about 6 GB of memory; the smallest runs on 2 GB.
  • Bengali voice typing depends on your phone; many phones only offer English offline.
  • Android only for now, from GitHub. Not on the Play Store yet.

Download for Android View the source
  1. Download the APK

    Open the release page on your phone and tap the file ending in arm64-v8a.apk.

  2. Allow the install

    Android asks once to allow installs from your browser. Allow it, then tap Install.

  3. Pick a model

    Emberwit checks your phone and suggests a model. Download it, ideally on Wi-Fi.

  4. Go offline

    That’s it. Turn the internet off and keep chatting.

Needs Android 8.0 or newer, a 64-bit phone, and at least 2 GB of memory. Each release lists SHA-256 checksums so you can verify the file.
